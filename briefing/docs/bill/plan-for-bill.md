# In one page {drawing=two-chairs}

::: lede
The purpose is simple: more of the right retirement conversations with you. Held, useful conversations with people who have already seen how you think, and, where the fit is mutual, client relationships that follow.
:::

People meet your way of thinking first, at their own pace: a free guide, a four-part workshop where your recorded explanations sit beside a simple tool, and a live game you host, the Retirement Crossroads Challenge. From any page, they can go straight to a free 15-minute conversation with you. Nobody has to climb a ladder of steps before they can ask you a question.

::: two-col rule
### What it asks of you

- Answers to ten questions, once, in one sitting.
- A 20-minute recorded conversation about how you explain things.
- Recording sessions, in batches, not every week.
- The weekly meeting slots you choose.
- Hosting Crossroads about once a month, if it earns its place.
- A short weekly voice note, and reading drafts.

+++

### What stays yours

- **Your words.** Nothing goes out in your name until you and your firm approve that exact version.
- **Your face and voice.** Real recordings only. No synthetic copy, ever, without your consent.
- **Your clients.** Only you advise. No AI answers anyone.
- **Your calendar and prices.** You set the capacity and the book's price.
- **Your accounts and content.** Opened by you or your firm, and yours at handover.
:::

::: callout title="Where it stands today"
The groundwork is done and checked: a full inventory, the rules everyone builds to, and a working version of the site on a test machine. **Nothing is live. Nothing has been bought, sent or published.** Friday 2 October can only be a clearly labelled rehearsal with made-up data, unless your approvals are recorded and your recordings exist.
:::

# What a visitor will experience {drawing=workshop-notebook}

## The Workshop Journey

A four-part exercise people do on their own time: your recorded explanation beside a simple tool. The chapters: **the life they want to fund**, **the income already planned**, **what they have built** and **when the pieces change**. They can pause, go back, skip what they don't know, and never upload a document. You record general explanations once; their own numbers appear on screen.

It ends with a summary they can print: what it shows, what it cannot show, and one good next question, with the guide and the 15-minute conversation on offer. Never a verdict such as "you can retire", never an alarm. **What they type never leaves their own device.**

## The Retirement Crossroads Challenge

A live, game-show-style session you host on Google Meet: ten fictional households, answers A, B, C or D, your explanation after each case, then questions. Proposed: 60&nbsp;minutes, with Arnaud moderating the chat and handling technical problems. Scores are game points, not a readiness test; it is recorded only if you approve. <!-- ok: sixty -->

# What will never happen in your name {drawing=lighthouse}

::: lede
These are fixed rules of the plan. They change only by a written decision that you and Arnaud both make; no software or AI agent can change them.
:::

::: callout tone=rule title="Never"
- **Invented stories or credentials.** No made-up client stories, testimonials, quotations, reviews, rankings or titles.
- **An AI answering your clients or giving advice.** No chatbot talks to visitors, and a personal question goes to a person. The only AI task once the system runs is a first draft of the weekly article, which a person checks and you and your reviewer approve.
- **Fear or fake urgency.** No false deadlines, no alarms, no answer held back to push someone to book.
- **Publishing without your approval.** Nothing is published until you and your firm's reviewer approve that exact version. A material change afterwards needs a new approval.
- **A synthetic copy of your voice or face.** Real recordings only; ink drawings until photo rights are confirmed.
- **Pretending you looked at someone's file.** When a workshop clip is picked from their answers, the page says it was picked automatically and that you have not seen them.
- **Scraped lists or cold outreach.** No scraped contacts, automated cold messages or engagement bots.
:::

## How the rules are kept

Automated checks flag forbidden wording, and a separate AI checker that did none of the work reviews each piece. Those are technical checks. The approval is always human: you for your statements, your firm's reviewer for anything regulated, Arnaud for accounts and spending, each recorded with a name, a date and the exact version. A "yes" in a chat is not an approval. Nothing is switched on, bought or sent without that record, and each part has its own switch.

# Questions you might ask {drawing=dock}

### Will AI talk to my clients?

No. No chatbot, no AI replying to anyone. Reminders and receipts use fixed templates you and your reviewer approve. A reply with a personal question pauses the automated messages and goes to a person; personal financial questions are yours.

### Do I have to film every week?

No. Recording is planned in three batches; the two videos a week come from what you have already recorded. What is weekly is a short voice note and reading drafts.

### Can I say no to a script?

Yes, always. Scripts stay drafts until you approve and record them. If your own wording is better on the day, it is kept; a change of substance goes back to review.

### What if something breaks?

The part that failed is paused, with any advertising pointing to it; the guide and your contact details stay up where it is safe. A payment, email or publication is checked before it is repeated. Data is backed up nightly off the server, and a restore is rehearsed monthly. During Crossroads, Arnaud handles the technical side.

### Who sees people's information?

As few as possible. Workshop numbers never leave the visitor's device. The system keeps an email, an optional first name, the language, what the person asked for and agreed to receive. Card details stay with the payment service, booking details with your calendar. Before any real personal data is used, the person responsible for privacy decides the providers, retention and consent wording (HB-21, HB-27).

### What happens if nobody signs up?

We find the step where people stop and change one thing at a time. Nothing is bought in the hope of sign-ups; Crossroads continues only while attendance justifies it. No forecast is given, because no source supports one. What you record stays yours.
